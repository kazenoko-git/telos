Calling nn.utils.clip_grad_norm_ on lazy XLA tensors without proper graph evaluation or barrier creates unreduced XLA subgraphs across the 8 TPU cores.
Bypassing PyTorch-XLA's canonical optimizer tracking (xm.optimizer_step) caused parameter updates to fall out of step with the PJRT execution graph, causing loss drift starting at step ~7,000 and total divergence by step 21,000.
B. bfloat16 Underflow in AR Cross-Entropy
In 

telos/diffusion/ar.py
 (Commit 17ec14a):

python
shift_logits = logits[:, :-1, :].contiguous().view(-1, vocab_size)
shift_targets = batch_seqs[:, 1:].contiguous().view(-1)
# shift_logits is in bfloat16 under torch.autocast("xla", torch.bfloat16)

Under TPU XLA autocast, shift_logits is in bfloat16 (7-bit mantissa). Computing cross-entropy over a vocabulary of 8,192 tokens in raw bfloat16 without upcasting to float32 causes severe underflow and numerical instability in the log-sum-exp reduction.
CUDA handles this natively in cuDNN; PyTorch-XLA requires explicit shift_logits.float().
C. Lack of Bidirectional Entropy Regularization
In COROSred (both 15M and 50M), 25% of the batch uses bidirectional attention for masked token infilling, and the LRH head computes binary cross-entropy on token certainty. This prevents logits from collapsing into repetitive whitespace or punctuation attractors. Pure AR had no such anchor.

I have completed the repository-wide audit and formulated the remediation plan.

### Summary of Key Findings

1. **PyTorch-XLA Optimizer Stepping (`trainer_pytorch.py`)**:
   - In commit `b286a90`, `xm.optimizer_step(self.optimizer)` was bypassed under the mistaken belief that calling `xm.reduce_gradients()` followed by `xm.optimizer_step()` would scale gradients by $1/\text{world\_size}^2$ ($1/64\times$ on 8 cores).
   - In reality, an all-reduce mean on identical tensors across all replicas is **strictly idempotent** ($\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N g = g$).
   - Bypassing `xm.optimizer_step()` prevented PyTorch-XLA from tracking parameter updates in the PJRT device execution graph, accumulating unreduced lazy subgraphs and causing the loss drift starting at step ~7,000 and total divergence by step 21,000.
   - Similar bypasses were discovered in `scripts/colab/train_25m_upscaled.py` and `scripts/lightning/train_25m_lightning.py`.

2. **Repository-Wide bfloat16 Log-Sum-Exp Underflow**:
   - In `telos/diffusion/ar.py`, `shift_logits` was passed to `F.cross_entropy` in raw `bfloat16`. Over an 8,192-token vocabulary, raw `bfloat16` (7-bit mantissa) causes underflow during log-sum-exp reductions on TPU MXUs.
   - **Critical Extension**: This underflow issue was also present across **all other loss modules** in the repo:
     - `telos/diffusion/corosred_unified.py` (causal CE, infill CE, and LRH BCE)
     - `telos/diffusion/corosred.py` (all phases)
     - `telos/diffusion/mdlm.py`
     - `telos/diffusion/undlm.py`
     - `telos/training/monitor.py`
     - `scripts/eval_50m_suite.py` & `scripts/eval_50m_head_to_head.py`

3. **100-Step Sanity Probe & Documentation**:
