* The H100 SXM5 has **132 Streaming Multiprocessors (SMs)**.
* Hopper's 4th-Gen Tensor Cores (DPX/MMA instructions) execute hardware tiles of size $[128 \times 128 \times 64]$.
* For a 50M model, the hidden dimension is only **$d_{\text{model}} = 512$** (with head dimension $d_{\text{head}} = 32$).

* The GPU finishes computing the tile before the next warp scheduler can even pipeline the subsequent memory fetch. * **GEMMs** (projections) have high arithmetic intensity ($\text{FLOPs} / \text{byte}$).

* In a 70B model, GEMMs represent $>99\%$ of the execution time.
* In a 50M model, memory-bound elementwise operations and activations represent $\sim 30–40\%$ of the total step latency.
