### 2. Why Does COROSReD Beat AR at AR's Own Game (Causal Prediction)?

One would expect a bidirectional diffusion model to be good at infilling, but why did causal next-token perplexity drop from **$414 \rightarrow 28$**?

1. **Self-Correction as a Structural Regularizer**:
   * Pure AR training only optimizes for maximum likelihood on prefix $x_{<t} \rightarrow x_t$. If the model makes an error during autoregressive generation, it suffers from severe exposure bias and compounding drift.
   * In Phase B, COROSReD generates its own draft predictions and uses the Learned Reliability Head to locate its mistakes, forcing the network to repair semantic and syntactic errors using bidirectional attention.
   * This forces the transformer to internalize global scoping, variable lifecycles, and function signatures. When you evaluate it in pure causal mode (`mask_override=True`), those deep structural representations drastically improve next-token prediction.

2. **No Exposure Bias**:
   * AR never sees corruptions during training. COROSReD was trained on $50\%$ noisy draft states, making it resilient to imperfect preceding code context.
