### Root Cause of the DDP Conflict & Checkpoint Metadata Mismatch

1. **Why `use_reentrant=True` threw `RuntimeError: Expected to mark a variable ready only once`**:
   * Reentrant checkpointing re-enters the autograd engine multiple times per layer during backward.
   * Under PyTorch DDP, this causes parameter gradient synchronization hooks to fire multiple times for the same parameter (e.g. `layers.15.mlp.w2.weight`), which DDP rejects. DDP requires `use_reentrant=False`.

2. **Why `use_reentrant=False` failed earlier with `CheckpointError`**:
   * In COROSRED Phase B, each step first executes a **draft pass** inside `with torch.no_grad():`.
   * Under `torch.amp.autocast`, this draft pass populated PyTorch's internal autocast cache with tensors created with `requires_grad=False`.
   * Furthermore, because `self.training` was True, `torch.utils.checkpoint` was being needlessly called during the draft pass (triggering `UserWarning: None of the inputs have requires_grad=True. Gradients will be None`).
   * When the subsequent training forward pass ran with `use_reentrant=False`, its unpack hook collided with the dirty autocast cache, causing the shape/metadata mismatch.
