### Why the 85% Raw Accuracy Was Distorted

Under token-level pretraining at early/mid stages, top-1 next-token prediction accuracy is $\approx 10\%$. This creates an extreme **$9:1$ class imbalance**:
- True negative tokens ($y = 0$, model was wrong): $\sim 90\%$
- True positive tokens ($y = 1$, model was correct): $\sim 10\%$

Under this distribution, a degenerate predictor that outputs a constant negative score ($r_i < 0$) gets **$\approx 90\%$ raw classification accuracy** despite having **zero discriminative capability**. The previous threshold `lrh_acc >= 0.65` was therefore opening prematurely on step 1.

By switching the gate to **ROC-AUC / Balanced Accuracy**:
- Chance level (random or constant prediction) is strictly **$0.50$**, completely invariant to the $9:1$ class ratio.
- An $\text{EMA}_{\text{auc}} \ge 0.55$ threshold guarantees that the gate only opens when the head demonstrates genuine positive separation ($\text{AUC} > 0.50$).
- This directly aligns with the `mask_blend` schedule scale ($\text{auc\_gate\_min} = 0.50$, $\text{auc\_gate\_target} = 0.75$).

#### 2. telos/diffusion/corosred_unified.py
- Implemented static 0-dimensional tensor-native **Balanced Accuracy**:
  $$\text{TPR} = \frac{\sum (\hat{y}=1 \land y=1)}{\sum (y=1)}, \quad \text{TNR} = \frac{\sum (\hat{y}=0 \land y=0)}{\sum (y=0)}$$
  $$\text{Balanced Acc} = \frac{\text{TPR} + \text{TNR}}{2}$$
  This operation is 100% TPU/XLA static-graph compatible (no `.cpu()` synchronization, no dynamic tensor slicing), evaluates to $0.50$ on degenerate imbalance, and serves as an in-step proxy for AUC on accelerators.
