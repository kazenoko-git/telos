### 3. Why Did This Happen? (Is It an Evaluation Config Mismatch?)

**It is NOT an evaluation script bug** (like an off-by-one indexing error or mismatched mask token ID). 
The exact same evaluation runner runner.py:150-175 evaluates 100M COROSred at **79.2% Top-5 accuracy** and CE = 2.40 on the exact same token indices.

Instead, the cause is an **Architectural Training-Objective Entanglement**:

#### The Causal Next-Token Shortcut
1. In autoregressive language modeling, position $i$ is trained to predict token $i+1$. The output head at position $i$ is wired to produce token $i+1$.
2. Under causal masking, position $i$ cannot see position $i+1$, so it is forced to synthesize prior context.
3. If causal masking is disabled (`mask_override=False`), position $i$ can directly attend to position $i+1$ via RoPE relative distance $+1$.
4. Any head that attends to position $i+1$ copies token $i+1$'s embedding directly into the residual stream. Because the output projection is trained to output "what comes next", the model takes the path of least resistance: **it directly copies token $i+1$ into the logits**.

#### Why 50M COROSred Fell Into This Trap
In corosred_unified.py:201-214:
- **Causal batch ($B_c = 36$ seqs)**: All tokens are supervised ($36 \times 511 \approx 18,396$ tokens per step).
- **Infill batch ($B_m = 12$ seqs)**: Only 15% of tokens are masked ($12 \times 512 \times 0.15 \approx 921$ tokens per step).
- Even with per-token loss pooling, the **gradient flow through the shared transformer backbone is ~20:1 dominated by the causal task**.
- During training on full 512-token sequences with 15% uniform random noise, the model learned coarse bag-of-words denoising (`val_bidir` CE = 6.62). But on **isolated single-token masks in short code sequences**, the dominant causal circuit takes over. The model never had enough bidirectional supervision or capacity to learn:
  > *"When the current token is `[MASK]`, break the causal $+1$ copy head and attend backward to the prompt."*

#### Why 100M COROSred Escaped
At 100M parameters and 5.0B tokens (25,432 steps, 4.3x higher training compute budget), the network had sufficient capacity and step count for the bidirectional denoising circuits to specialize and actively decouple from the causal copy heads. As shown in the attention table above, 100M learned to suppress the $i+1$ lookahead head down to 3.5% in Layer 13 and route 70.6% of its attention back to the prefix.
