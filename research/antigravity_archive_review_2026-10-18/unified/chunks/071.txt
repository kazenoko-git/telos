### 1. The Core Difference: Bidirectional Infill vs. Causal Continuation

The probe suite (telos/eval/probes.py) defines each probe with three parts:
* **`prompt` (prefix)**: e.g. `def __init__(self,`
* **`target`**: e.g. `name`
* **`suffix`**: e.g. `):\n        self.name = name`

Currently, `evaluate_probes` in telos/eval/runner.py constructs:
$$\text{input} = [\text{prompt}] + [\text{MASK}] + [\text{suffix}]$$
and runs with **`mask_override=False` (Bidirectional attention)**.

* **This is a pure Infill test**: The model sees both past and future code to predict the middle token.
* **AR models cannot do this**: Because AR models only have a causal mask and no `[MASK]` token training, feeding bidirectional context causes their attention to collapse (scoring 0.0% Top-1 / 12.05 CE).

### Issue 1: Why 100M AR's Infill CE (12.05) is Worse Than Random Guessing (~9.01)

#### The Intuition vs. Information Theory
For a vocabulary of $V = 8,192$, a **uniform random guess** ($P(w) = \frac{1}{8,192} \approx 1.22 \times 10^{-4}$) gives:
$$\text{CE}_{\text{uniform}} = -\ln\left(\frac{1}{8,192}\right) = \ln(8,192) \approx 9.01\text{ nats}$$

Cross-entropy loss $\text{CE} = -\ln P(y_{\text{target}})$ is **unbounded above** $[0, \infty)$. A model performs *worse than random guessing* if it is **confidently wrong**—meaning it concentrates high probability mass on the wrong tokens, actively starving the true target token of probability.

#### What Actually Happens in 100M AR Under Bidirectional Attention:
1. **Attention Collapse**: 
   AR models are trained with a strictly lower-triangular causal mask ($M_{ij} = -\infty$ for $j > i$). When given full unmasked attention (`mask_override=False`), query-key pairs in the 14 layers attend to future tokens (newlines, `def`, `pass`, colons).
2. **Sharply Peaked on Spurious Attractors**:
   Instead of flattening into a maximum-entropy uniform distribution ($\approx 9.01$), the model's output entropy collapses to **4.64 nats**. The top-1 spurious token alone (often newline `Ċ` or `(`) captures **20% to 27% of the entire probability mass**.
3. **Severe Suppression of the Target Token**:
   Because the probability is concentrated on 1–2 spurious attractors, the true target token's probability is actively suppressed down to $10^{-8}$ or $10^{-9}$:
   * On probe `class [MASK]: def __init__(self, lr=1e-3)...` (target `Config`): $P(\text{target}) = 6.62 \times 10^{-9} \implies \mathbf{\text{CE} = 18.83}$
   * On probe `torch.[MASK].is_available()...` (target `cuda`): $P(\text{target}) = 1.29 \times 10^{-8} \implies \mathbf{\text{CE} = 18.17}$
   * In **84.2% of the probes**, AR's target token probability is strictly lower than uniform random ($1.22 \times 10^{-4}$).
   
   These high-loss outliers pull the suite average up to **12.05**.
4. **Why 50M AR Was Closer to 9.01 (8.90)**:
   The 50M model has smaller hidden dimension (512 vs 768) and lower capacity; under corrupting attention, its logits are flatter and less confident, keeping its CE near the uniform baseline of ~9.
