### Root Cause of the 14 GB Memory Usage

On Turing T4 GPUs (compute capability 7.5), FlashAttention 2 is not supported. In **Phase B (bidirectional attention)**, PyTorch's SDPA materializes the full $[B, H, S, S]$ attention score matrix—which is **384 MiB per layer**.

* **Why TPU never had this problem**: In `telos/train/cli.py` line 192, gradient checkpointing was hardcoded to auto-enable **only for TPU** (`device == "xla"`).
* **Why CUDA OOM'd at both 192 and 96**: On CUDA, gradient checkpointing was **disabled**. PyTorch had to retain all 8 layers of 384 MiB attention matrices simultaneously for the backward pass, consuming 14.00 GiB before step 1 even finished.

---

### What Was Updated

1. **`telos/train/cli.py`**: Auto-enable `use_grad_checkpoint` on CUDA as well (`device in ["xla", "cuda"]`).
2. **`telos/training/trainer_pytorch.py`**: Ensure `auto_chkpt` evaluates to `True` when `self.device.type == "cuda"`.

With gradient checkpointing enabled, each layer recomputes attention on-the-fly during backward. **VRAM drops from 14.00 GiB to ~2.8 GiB**, meaning **`batch_size = 192` with `grad_accum = 1`** now fits comfortably on 15 GB T4s!

All 22 unit tests pass.
