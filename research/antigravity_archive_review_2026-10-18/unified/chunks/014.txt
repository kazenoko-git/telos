### 1. The Math in telos/configs/builder.py
[fenced code/output: original retained]
`builder.py` resolves effective batch strictly as:
$$\text{effective\_batch} = \text{batch\_size} \times \text{gradient\_accumulation}$$
`devices` is never factored into that formula.

---

### 2. How PyTorch-XLA SPMD Handles the Batch
In Telos, multi-core TPU training uses PyTorch-XLA **SPMD** (telos/training/trainer_pytorch.py):
[fenced code/output: original retained]
In SPMD:
- **`batch_size` is the GLOBAL batch size across all chips**, not the per-core batch size.
- When `batch_seqs` has shape `(384, 512)` and is sharded across the 8-core mesh, XLA automatically splits dimension 0 so that:
  $$\frac{384 \text{ global batch}}{8 \text{ cores}} = \mathbf{48 \text{ sequences per core}}$$

---

### 3. What Happens If You Lower It to 48:
- If you set `--batch-size 48 --effective-batch 384`:
  - `batch_size` = 48 (global) $\to$ each TPU core receives only $\frac{48}{8} = \mathbf{6}$ sequences per step, severely underutilizing the TPU MXU systolic arrays.
  - `grad_accum` would become $\lceil \frac{384}{48} \rceil = 8$ steps.
