1. **LibTPU Host Spin-Waiting (Busy-Polling)**:
   - **Cause**: By default, Google's `libtpu.so` driver aggressively busy-polls host CPU cores in tight loops waiting for TPU HBM completion registers. With 8 spawned workers, 8 host threads were pegged in busy-wait spinloops ($\sim 800\%$ CPU).

2. **OpenMP Multi-Core Thread Storm**:
