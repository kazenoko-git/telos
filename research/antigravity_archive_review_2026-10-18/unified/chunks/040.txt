**The PyTorch-XLA Failure Mode:**
1. **Lazy Graph Aliasing**: In PyTorch-XLA, operations are not executed immediately—they are queued in an asynchronous HLO graph. When `self.optimizer.step()` is called on XLA master tensors `mp`, the computation is still **lazy**.
