* **Steps 0 $\rightarrow$ 12,714 (Tokens 0 $\rightarrow$ 2.5B)**:

* **Step 12,715 (Token 2.5B)**:
  * `idx_ptr` hits $n_{\text{rows}}$ and wraps around to `0`.
* **Steps 12,715 $\rightarrow$ 20,346 (Tokens 2.5B $\rightarrow$ 4.0B = remaining 1.5B tokens)**:
