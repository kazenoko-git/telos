The failure of the 100M AR model on TPU traces back to a fundamental incompatibility between **PyTorch's `use_reentrant=False` gradient checkpointing mechanism** and **PyTorch-XLA's lazy computational graph engine**.

Here is the exact technical breakdown of why this fault occurred:

---

### 1. The Core Conflict: Autograd Hooks vs. XLA Lazy Tracing

In PyTorch 2.x, `use_reentrant=False` is implemented using **`torch.autograd.graph.saved_tensors_hooks`**:
- During the forward pass, instead of stashing layer activations in HBM, PyTorch discards them and stores a placeholder closure with the input tensors (`h`, `cos`, `sin`).
- During the backward pass, when the autograd engine reaches that layer, the `unpack_hook` dynamically **re-executes the forward function** in Python to regenerate the activations just-in-time for computing gradients.

#### Why this breaks on PyTorch-XLA (TPU)
On CUDA, ops run eagerly. On TPU, PyTorch-XLA does not execute operations eagerly; it records tensor operations into a **High-Level Optimizer (HLO) computation graph**.

1. **Subgraph Duplication Inside Autograd**: When the autograd engine triggers the recomputation forward pass during backward, PyTorch-XLA records these re-executed forward operations as **new nodes in the backward HLO graph**.
2. **Out-of-Order Graph Interleaving Across TPU Replicas**:
   - Autograd visits nodes in topological order based on ready queues. Across 8 independent PJRT TPU worker processes, slight microsecond execution skews cause the recomputation hooks to fire in subtly non-deterministic orders relative to other graph ops.
   - This creates mismatched HLO graph signatures and out-of-order execution paths across the 8 TPU cores.
3. **Gradient Decoupling (The "Pseudo-Convergence" Trap)**:
   - Because of the recomputation hook dispatch inside the lazy backward pass, gradients flowed through corrupted intermediate references.
   - The backward pass still produced finite floating-point numbers (no `NaN` or `Inf`), which is why the job didn't crash with a floating-point exception.
   - **However, the computed gradients were essentially decorrelated noise relative to the true forward loss surface.**

---
