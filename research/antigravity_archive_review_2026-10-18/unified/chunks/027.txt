1. **Bidirectional Infilling Power**:
   - In **COROSReD without LRH**, the Phase B model was trained with true bidirectional attention (`mask_override=False`). 3. **Where the LRH Fits In**:
   - **COROSReD without LRH** relies on uniform random masking: 15% of tokens are masked at random regardless of whether the causal draft already knew them with 99% certainty.
   - **COROSReD with LRH** is designed to use the scalar reliability scores ($r_i$) to concentrate the bidirectional denoiser's compute exclusively on ambiguous or erroneous tokens. Passing explicit `mask_override=False` in MLX Pass 2 will allow it to match the $3.39$ CE bidirectional baseline while gaining error-selective routing.
