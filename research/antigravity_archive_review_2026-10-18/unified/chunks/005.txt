   - On Google Cloud / Kaggle TPU VMs (TPU v3-8), the host communicates with each TPU chip through Linux IOMMU VFIO character device nodes: `/dev/vfio/0`, `/dev/vfio/1`, `/dev/vfio/2`, `/dev/vfio/3`.
