What is microbatch size?

[L1901 S1912 PLANNER_RESPONSE 2026-08-08T04:51:07Z ]
In deep learning training, **microbatch size** is the number of sequences processed in GPU/unified memory during a single forward-and-backward pass before accumulating gradients.

---

### 💡 **How Microbatching Works in Your Telos Setup**

In your active training configs (`configs/phase_b_50m_1to25_mlx.yaml`):

- **Microbatch Size (`microbatch_size`)**: **`16`**
  - Your Metal GPU processes **16 sequences** at a time in memory during one forward/backward pass.
- **Gradient Accumulation Steps (`gradient_accumulation_steps`)**: **`16`**
  - The trainer loops **16 microbatches** in a row, accumulating the gradients without updating weights yet.
- **Effective Batch Size (`batch_size`)**: **`256`** ($16 \times 16$)
  - After accumulating 16 microbatches ($16 \times 16 = 256$ sequences / $524,288$ tokens), the optimizer performs **1 weight update step**.

---

### 🎯 **Why We Use Microbatching**

1. **Memory Ceiling Control**: 
   Fitting a full batch of 256 sequences ($524k$ tokens) into Metal unified memory all at once would require over **20+ GB of VRAM** and trigger severe system swap.
2. **Identical Math, Fraction of RAM**: 
   By splitting the effective batch of 256 into **16 microbatches of 16**, the mathematical result on the weights is **100% identical** to training with a batch of 256, but memory usage is capped at only **~0.41 GB – 0.83 GB** of active RAM.
