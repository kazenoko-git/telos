> **Why Single-Step for Probes?**

### Why 1-Token Probes Collapse to 1 Step vs. Where Multi-Step Iterative Denoising Actually Functions

### 1. The Mathematical Reason for 1-Token Probes
In the 100 contextual probes suite, every probe tests a **single target token** (e.g., `return a +` $\to$ target: `b`):

- In MDLM, if there is only **1 masked slot** $[M]$:
  $$\text{Input: } [x_1, x_2, \dots, x_T, [M]]$$
  Whether you run the sampler for **1 step or 64 steps**, the schedule simply decides *at which step* that single slot unmasks. When it finally unmasks, it evaluates the exact distribution:
  $$p_\theta(x_0^{(T+1)} \mid x_{1:T}, [M])$$
- Because there are no *other* masked tokens to progressively reveal and cross-condition on, **a 1-token iterative diffusion loop is mathematically identical to a single-step evaluation.**

### 2. Where Multi-Step Iterative Generation Matters: Multi-Token Spans

Multi-step iterative denoising creates its advantage when infilling **multi-token spans** (e.g., 8 to 32 tokens):

$$\text{Input: } [x_1, \dots, x_T, [M], [M], [M], [M], [M], [M], [M], [M]]$$

1. **Step 1 (High Noise)**: The model inspects all 8 slots simultaneously. It is most confident about high-entropy syntactic anchors (e.g., `:` at slot 8, `return` at slot 1) and locks them in first.
2. **Step 2 (Medium Noise)**: In the second forward pass, the model now sees the concrete anchors filled in from Step 1. Its bidirectional attention uses both the prefix and the anchored suffix to resolve identifiers and operators in slots 2–7.
3. **Step 3 (Low Noise)**: Fine-grained details (argument names, literals) are finalized.
4. **For UNDLM (Self-Correction)**: If a token selected in Step 1 becomes inconsistent with the syntax chosen in Step 2, the model overwrites and self-corrects it.

### 3. Multi-Token Iterative Evaluation Benchmark

To benchmark the multi-step iterative sampler directly against Autoregressive generation, we evaluate **Multi-Token Span Infilling**:

| Evaluation Type | Target Length | Decoding Mechanism | Metrics Evaluated |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Contextual Probes** (`--mode probes`) | 1 Token | Direct Logit Evaluation | Target Rank, Cross-Entropy Loss, Top-1/Top-5 Hit Rate |
| **Multi-Token Iterative Suite** (`--mode sample`) | 8–64 Tokens | Iterative Sampler ($N=64$ steps) | Exact Match (EM), Token Accuracy, Syntax Validity (`ast.parse`), BLEU / Edit Distance |

The model has **never seen** a sequence that is:
1. Only **5–15 tokens long** (training uses 512-token sequences)
2. Has exactly **1 masked token at the very end** with all other tokens clean
