### 1. Do We Need to Retrain the 12.5M Models?

**NO, ABSOLUTELY NOT.**

Here is the mathematical and architectural reason:
- **The Training Objective is Invariant**: The UNDLM training loss is the categorical variational bound (ELBO):
  $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{ELBO}} = \mathbb{E}_{t \in (0, 1],\, x_0 \sim q(x_0),\, x_t \sim q(x_t \mid x_0, t)} \left[ \frac{1}{t} \text{CrossEntropy}\left(x_0, \, p_\theta(x_0 \mid x_t, t)\right) \right]$$
- The neural network weights parameterize the clean token denoiser $p_\theta(x_0 \mid x_t)$.
- **Self-Correction vs. Re-corruption is purely an INFERENCE / SAMPLING algorithm**: The difference between injecting blind uniform noise versus performing iterative confidence-guided token revision and self-correction occurs entirely inside the inference sampler. The 12.5M pretrained weights in `checkpoints/uniform/12m/` are the **exact denoiser distribution $p_\theta(x_0 \mid x_t)$ needed for self-corrective decoding**.
