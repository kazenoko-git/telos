### Mathematically Principled Evaluation Protocols for UNDLM

To evaluate UNDLM without structural penalty, we have three valid protocols:

#### Protocol 1: Monte Carlo Uniform Marginalization (Recommended for zero-shot probes)
Instead of a fixed `[MASK]` token, sample $K$ uniform random tokens $r_1, \dots, r_K \sim \text{Uniform}(0, |\mathcal{V}| - 1)$ at the target position, and compute the expected posterior distribution:
$$\bar{p}_\theta(x_0^{\text{target}} \mid \text{prompt}) = \frac{1}{K} \sum_{k=1}^K \text{softmax}\Big(f_\theta\big([\text{prompt}, r_k]\big)_{\text{target}}\Big)$$

#### Protocol 2: Analytic Mean-Embedding Conditioning
Instead of sampling discrete tokens, substitute the input embedding at the target position with the expected uniform token embedding:
$$\bar{\mathbf{e}} = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{V}|} \sum_{v \in \mathcal{V}} \mathbf{E}[v]$$

#### Protocol 3: Unified ELBO Likelihood Evaluation

### 1. Apache 2.0 (The Industry Standard for AI Code)
