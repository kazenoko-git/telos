### **Root Cause**
In MLX's lazy evaluation engine, the gradient accumulation line:
```python
accum_grads = tree_map(lambda a, b: a + b, accum_grads, grads)
```
creates an **unevaluated graph node** for addition.

Because `mx.eval(accum_grads)` was not called *inside* the accumulation loop, MLX did not evaluate the sum dynamically. Instead, it built a **16-step computational graph** containing all backward activation trees, all model graph nodes, and all temporary allocation buffers for all 16 microbatches simultaneously. Over time, MLX's graph retention ballooned to **45+ GB**.

### **The Fix**
We now explicitly force MLX to evaluate the accumulated gradient tree on **every single microbatch iteration**:

```python
for i in range(grad_accum):

if accum_grads is None:
```
