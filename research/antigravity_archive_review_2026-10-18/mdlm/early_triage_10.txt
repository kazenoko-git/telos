### What's Happening (The "Compilation Thrashing" Bug)
PyTorch/XLA on TPU builds a static computation graph before it runs on the hardware. It usually takes ~30 to 60 seconds to compile this graph on step 1, and then every subsequent step uses the cached graph and takes ~0.1 seconds.
